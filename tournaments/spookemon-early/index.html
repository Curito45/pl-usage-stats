EVENT INFORMATION

Rules & Format

View tournament rulesBattle format, allowed mechanics, clauses and restrictions

Format

  • Singles · 6v6
  • National Dex OU (Standard Gym Rules)
  • Double elimination
  • October 31, 2026
  • Two separate tournaments on the same date

Rules

  • No Legendary, Mythical or Ultra Beast Pokémon
  • No Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, Dynamax or Terastallization
  • Item Clause: no duplicate held items
  • Every Pokémon must hold an approved Trick or Treat item
  • Maximum 3 Treat items per team
  • Treat items: Ghost-, Dark-, or Poison-type Pokémon only
  • Trick items may fill any remaining slots
  • Maximum 2 Pokémon subject to the Stall Clause
  • Additional item, move, Pokémon and Ability restrictions apply

⛓️ The Binding Curse — General restrictions Items, moves, abilities and suppression

Item Selection

  • Every Pokémon must hold an approved Trick or Treat item.
  • A maximum of 3 Treat items is allowed per team.
  • Treat items may only be held by Ghost-, Dark- or Poison-type Pokémon.
  • Any Pokémon not holding a Treat item must hold an approved Trick item.
  • No duplicate held items (Item Clause).
  • Each Pokémon must hold its assigned item at the start of battle.

Banned Moves

Trick, Switcheroo, Fling, Knock Off, Thief, Covet, Bestow and Corrosive Gas.

Banned Abilities

Magician, Pickpocket and Symbiosis.

Item Suppression

Embargo and Magic Room are banned.

Natural item consumption is allowed. Sticky Barb’s natural transfer effect is permitted as a specific exception to the item-transfer ban.
